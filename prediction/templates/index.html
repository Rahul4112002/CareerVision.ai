Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our AI-powered career prediction platform and how it can help you discover your ideal career path.

How does our AI predict career paths?

Our advanced machine learning algorithm analyzes your skills, interests, education, and personality traits using a neural network trained on 20,000+ career success stories to provide highly accurate predictions.

What data do we use for predictions?

We analyze 39 key factors including your academic performance, technical skills, soft skills, certifications, workshops, interests, and career preferences to create a comprehensive profile.

How accurate are the predictions?

Our model achieves over 90% accuracy in predicting suitable career paths. We continuously validate results with industry experts and real-world career success stories.

What benefits do students get?

Students receive personalized career predictions, skill gap analysis, learning resource recommendations, real-time job listings, and detailed roadmaps for their chosen career path.

Is the platform free to use?

Yes! Our career prediction platform is completely free to use. We believe everyone should have access to AI-powered career guidance regardless of their financial situation.

Can I change careers using this platform?

Absolutely! Our platform adapts to career transitions and changes. Whether you're a student, recent graduate, or considering a career switch, we provide relevant guidance for your situation.
